FAQ

Do I need a label?

No. Independent artists, labels and managers can launch campaigns.

Can I choose who promotes my music?

Yes. Choose the content creators you want to post with your track, invite them directly or review incoming offers, and confirm each completed publication before payment is released.

How much does a campaign cost?

You choose the campaign period and social media accounts. Each account has a fixed price per post, and the combined cost becomes your campaign budget.

When are content creators paid?

Content creators are paid after they publish the content and you confirm the post. The funds are then released to their account.

Can I withdraw unused funds?

Yes. Any unused reserved funds can be withdrawn after your promotion campaign ends.

Which platforms are supported?

Spotify, SoundCloud, TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

Who can see my track?

While your campaign is active, your track is visible to registered content creators who have linked at least one social media account.
