Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Musin (“Musin”, “we”, “us” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use the Musin website and platform.

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow websites and online services to perform certain functions, such as maintaining a logged-in session and supporting the security and proper operation of the service.

2. How Musin uses cookies

Musin currently uses essential cookies only.

These cookies are necessary for the website and platform to operate and may be used for purposes including:

  • Authentication and account access — to keep users securely signed in and maintain active sessions.
  • Security and platform integrity — to help protect accounts, prevent unauthorized access and support the secure operation of Musin.

Essential cookies cannot be used by Musin for advertising or behavioral tracking.

3. Analytics and advertising cookies

Musin does not currently use cookies for:

  • analytics or audience measurement;
  • personalized advertising;
  • cross-site behavioral tracking; or
  • marketing profiling.

We therefore do not place non-essential cookies that require your consent.

If Musin introduces analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies in the future, this Cookie Policy will be updated and, where required, users will be given appropriate cookie controls before those cookies are placed.

4. Third-party services

Certain features of Musin may rely on third-party service providers necessary to operate the platform, such as infrastructure, authentication, security or payment-related services.

Where such providers use cookies or similar technologies as part of providing a requested service, their use may also be governed by their own privacy and cookie policies.

Musin does not permit third-party advertising or behavioral tracking cookies on the platform unless this Policy is updated and any legally required consent is obtained.

5. How long are cookies stored?

The duration of an essential cookie depends on its purpose.

Some cookies are session cookies and are deleted when you close your browser. Others may remain on your device for a limited period where this is necessary for account security, authentication or another essential platform function.

Musin retains cookies only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they are used.

6. Managing cookies

Because Musin currently uses only cookies necessary for the operation and security of the service, disabling them may prevent certain parts of the website or platform from functioning correctly.

Most browsers allow you to view, delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Please note that blocking essential cookies may prevent you from signing in or using certain Musin features.

7. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy if our use of cookies or similar technologies changes, or where changes are required to reflect legal, technical or operational developments.

The date at the top of this Policy indicates when it was last updated.

If we begin using non-essential cookies that require consent, users will be provided with the appropriate information and controls before those cookies are activated.
