FAQ

How many followers do I need?

Just 100 followers are enough to get started. Musin is built for creators of all sizes.

Which platforms are supported?

Currently Musin supports: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Spotify, Stripe.

How does Musin verify my account?

You connect your account through an official login with Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube. This confirms your ownership and lets Musin show artists your verified follower and performance data, rather than numbers you’d have to self-report.

How do payments work?

A post is eligible for payment when it uses the correct track, is published within the campaign period, and follows the required track timeframe if one was set. When you submit your post link, Musin captures its publish date automatically. The artist can review and confirm any time before payout — this just marks it as dispute-free and doesn’t speed up or slow down payment. Whether or not they confirm, your payout is calculated and added to your balance 14 days after your post’s publish date, or when the campaign ends, whichever comes first. Campaigns run for a maximum of 30 days.

How is my payment calculated?

You set a price per post within the limit set by your follower count. Musin captures your post’s publish date automatically when you submit the link, then measures its performance 14 days later, or when the campaign ends, whichever comes first — looking at both how many people it reached relative to your follower count, and how many of them engaged (likes, comments, shares, saves) relative to the views. If both are weak for an account your size, you get 50% of your set price; if both are strong, you get 110%; everything else pays the full price. You’re never paid nothing for a completed, confirmed post.

How often can I withdraw my earnings?

You can withdraw your Musin balance once every 14 days. Earnings from confirmed posts build up in your balance between withdrawals, so you can cash out everything you’ve earned in one transfer.

Do I have to post with every track?

No. You select tracks you like to use in your content.

How can I find tracks to use?

You can search for available music or receive tracks directly, then select the ones that you like.

Do I need to worry about music licensing?

No. Musin gives you a platform-cleared direct sound link for every track you select, so your post uses properly licensed audio for commercial use.

Is it free?

Yes. Creating an account and finding tracks is completely free. Musin charges a 20% commission only when you receive payment for a confirmed post.

How are payouts handled?

All payouts are processed securely through Stripe and sent in your local currency where supported.
