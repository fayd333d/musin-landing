Features

Musin connects music with the content creators who post it. Artists run campaigns and choose who takes part; creators choose the tracks they want to use and get paid for each confirmed post.

Campaigns

Artists upload a track, set the campaign period, budget and the fixed payment per post, and choose which social platforms the track may be posted on.

Campaign length

Campaigns run for up to 30 days, keeping budgets and payout timing predictable for artists and creators alike.

Mutual selection

Artists choose which accounts they want posting with their track, and creators choose which tracks they actually want to use. A collaboration only starts when both sides agree.

Creator discovery

Browse creators by platform, audience size, content category, language and posting price, so a track reaches accounts that genuinely suit it.

Verified accounts

Every creator connects their account through an official platform login, confirming real ownership and giving artists verified follower and performance data before they choose.

Track discovery

Creators browse open campaigns and pick music that fits their content, rather than being assigned a brief.

Creative freedom

Campaign instructions are guidance, not conditions. The one requirement is that the post uses the correct track, published to the agreed account inside the campaign period.

Licensed audio

Musin supplies platform-cleared direct sound links for every campaign track, so posts use properly licensed audio for commercial content.

Performance-adjusted payment

Each post has a base price agreed up front. Payout is measured 14 days after the post’s publish date, or when the campaign ends, whichever comes first (campaigns run up to 30 days), based on how the post’s reach and engagement compare to what’s typical for that account’s size and platform — never less than 50% of the agreed price, never more than 110%. A creator always knows that range before accepting.

Post confirmation

Creators submit the published link through Musin. The artist checks it against the agreed conditions, and confirmed posts move the payment to the creator’s balance.

Chats

Direct messaging between artists and creators, so a collaboration can be discussed in one place instead of across separate inboxes.

Notifications

Alerts for campaign invitations, submitted posts awaiting review, confirmations and payments, on the platform and by email.

Dispute review

The artist can confirm a post or raise a dispute any time before payout — confirming early doesn’t release payment sooner, and staying silent doesn’t delay it. If a post is disputed, Musin reviews the objective facts — the link, the timing, the account and the track used. Creative taste is never grounds for withholding payment, and performance only affects payout amount through the agreed reach and engagement tiers, never through subjective judgment.

Campaign results

Track posts, views, engagements and spend across a campaign as they come in.

Payments and withdrawals

Balances are paid out through Stripe. Musin takes a 20% commission when a creator withdraws, and a 10% platform fee on funds an artist deposits into a campaign. Creator withdrawals can be made once every 14 days. Joining is free with no listing fees or subscriptions.
