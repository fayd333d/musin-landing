Features

Musin connects music with the content creators who post it. Artists run campaigns and choose who takes part; creators choose the tracks they want to use and get paid a fixed amount per confirmed post.

Campaigns

Artists upload a track, set the campaign period, budget and the fixed payment per post, and choose which social platforms the track may be posted on.

Mutual selection

Artists choose which accounts they want posting with their track, and creators choose which tracks they actually want to use. A collaboration only starts when both sides agree.

Creator discovery

Browse creators by platform, audience size, content category, language and posting price, so a track reaches accounts that genuinely suit it.

Track discovery

Creators browse open campaigns and pick music that fits their content, rather than being assigned a brief.

Creative freedom

Campaign instructions are guidance, not conditions. The one requirement is that the post uses the correct track, published to the agreed account inside the campaign period.

Fixed payment per post

Payment is agreed up front and does not depend on views, likes or streams. A creator knows what a post pays before accepting it.

Post confirmation

Creators submit the published link through Musin. The artist checks it against the agreed conditions, and confirmed posts move the payment to the creator’s balance.

Chats

Direct messaging between artists and creators, so a collaboration can be discussed in one place instead of across separate inboxes.

Notifications

Alerts for campaign invitations, submitted posts awaiting review, confirmations and payments, on the platform and by email.

Dispute review

If a post is disputed, Musin reviews the objective facts — the link, the timing, the account and the track used. Creative taste and performance are never grounds for withholding payment.

Campaign results

Track posts, views, engagements and spend across a campaign as they come in.

Payments and withdrawals

Balances are paid out through Stripe. Musin takes a 20% commission when a creator withdraws, and joining is free with no listing fees or subscriptions.
