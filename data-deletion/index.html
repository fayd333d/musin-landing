Data Deletion Instructions

Musin, operated by Makarov d.o.o. (“we”, “us”), lets creators connect their Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube accounts to participate in music promotion campaigns. This page explains what account data we access through those platforms and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect from connected social accounts

When you connect Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube to Musin, we access:

  • your public profile information (username, display name, profile picture, follower count)
  • the public post/media links and engagement metrics you submit for campaign verification (likes, views, comment count on the specific post)

We do not access your private messages, private posts, or any content beyond what is needed to confirm a campaign post.

How to request deletion of your data

You can request deletion of your Musin account and all associated data collected from connected social accounts by:

  1. Emailing ask@musin.online with the subject “Data Deletion Request” from the email address associated with your Musin account, or
  2. Using the “Delete my account” option in Account Settings (if/once built).

What happens next

We will delete your account data, including data obtained via connected social platforms, within 30 days of a verified request. Some records may be retained longer where required by law (for example, financial and payment records tied to completed campaigns, per our Privacy Policy’s retention schedule).

Confirmation

Once deletion is complete, we will send a confirmation to the email address used to make the request.

Contact

Questions about this process: ask@musin.online
